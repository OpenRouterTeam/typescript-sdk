                                                                                                    | Whether the same request may be sent again unchanged. Always `true` for the transient refusals — `busy`, `intern_not_ready`, `intern_unreachable`, `rate_limited`, `stream_severed` and `timeout` — and always `false` for the ones a retry cannot fix. For `turn_failed` it varies by failure and is the intern's own classification of what went wrong: `true` for an upstream overload, rate limit, timeout or transport fault, `false` for an authentication or bad-request failure that would be rejected the same way again. Branch on this field rather than on `reason` when deciding whether to retry. A `429`, and a `409` or `503` with reason `busy`, also carry a `Retry-After` header saying how long to wait. |
